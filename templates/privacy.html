Privacy Policy

Last updated {{ last_updated }}. This policy covers the Marketplace Dashboard instance running at this domain.

What we collect

  • Account details — your email address and display name. If you sign up with a password, we store a bcrypt hash of it, never the password itself.
  • Marketplace connections — the OAuth access and refresh tokens for the marketplace accounts you choose to connect, plus any API keys you enter. These grant access to your own seller accounts.
  • Inventory data — listing titles, descriptions, prices, images, quantities, identifiers and status, synced from the marketplaces you connect.
  • Your own records — the costs, parts, notes and sales you enter.
  • Session data — a session token so you stay signed in between page loads.

How we use it

Only to run the dashboard for you: to display your listings, sync them from the marketplaces you connected, and calculate your costs, profit and margins. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Who can see it

You, and the members of any team you deliberately join. Inventory is scoped per team, so a different account on this instance cannot see your listings. Nobody else can read your marketplace connections — they are scoped to your account.

How it is stored

On this instance's own server. Marketplace access and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest, and the database is not exposed publicly. Traffic to this site is served over HTTPS. Database backups are taken on the same host.

Cookies

One HttpOnly session cookie to keep you signed in, and a short-lived HttpOnly cookie during a marketplace connection that protects the OAuth handshake. No advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

Deleting your data

You can remove listings and disconnect marketplace accounts at any time from My Accounts. {% if contact_email %} To have your account and all associated data erased, contact {{ contact_email }}. {% else %} To have your account and all associated data erased, contact the operator of this instance. {% endif %}

If you delete your eBay account, eBay notifies us directly and we delete the connection and tokens we hold for it. We do not keep a copy of deleted data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
