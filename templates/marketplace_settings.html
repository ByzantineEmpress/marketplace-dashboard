What do these credentials do?

Each marketplace issues these when you register an application in your own seller developer account. The app uses them to read your active listings so they can be tracked here.

They are stored encrypted, and a saved secret is never sent back to the browser — you will see only that a value is set. Leaving a field blank keeps what is already saved; to remove a saved value, open the field, type a single space, and save.
